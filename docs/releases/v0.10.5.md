# v0.10.5

**Released:** 2026-10-27 · **Branch:** `main` · **Previous:** [v0.10.4](v0.10.4.md) (2026-10-26).

A patch with one fix: **App hosts stop flapping to Cloudflare 526.** The LakeApp controller's Secret pass deleted
the App Ingress's certificate copy and edge client CA on every pass, and the Ingress step recreated them. Between the
two, ingress-nginx served its fake certificate and Cloudflare (Full strict) answered 526. Seen on
`customer-insights-dashboard.us.lake.live` ([#1045](https://github.com/oramasearch/orama-operator/issues/1045)).

## What it includes

| PR | Change |
|---|---|
| [#1046](https://github.com/oramasearch/orama-operator/pull/1046) | **The App's Secret pass keeps its Ingress's Secrets.** `deleteStaleLakeAppSecrets` deleted every Secret with the App's labels that its `wanted` set didn't name, including the certificate copies (`orama.cloud/tls-copy=true`) and `<workload>-edge-client-ca`, which `reconcileLakeAppIngress` labels the same way. It now skips both; `deleteLakeAppTLS` and the copy cleanup still remove them when the host goes. Present since the edge client CA joined App Ingresses (v0.6.2); every runtime App with a host was affected. Issue [#1045](https://github.com/oramasearch/orama-operator/issues/1045) |

Since v0.10.4, `dev` also holds YAML changes under `config/clusters/`, resources applied from their files and not
part of the operator image: app-runtime v0.1.2 on every Lake (`be769a7`); lake v0.7.2, then the v0.7.3 hotfix, then
lake v0.8.0 with agents on `gpt-6.1-sol` and agent-application v0.2.4 (`ec346c3`, `ff719da`, `ec6f753`); and the
`lake-nonprod` members on agent-application v0.2.6 and agent-experiments v0.2.2 (`3d5f95f`).

## Upgrading

No CRD or schema change. Roll the managing cluster, then every leaf's `operator.yaml` to `v0.10.5`. The App
Secrets stop being recreated on the operator's next pass; nothing else needs doing.
