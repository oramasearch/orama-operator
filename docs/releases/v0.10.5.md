# v0.10.5

**Released:** 2026-10-27 · **Branch:** `main` · **Previous:** [v0.10.4](v0.10.4.md) (2026-10-26).

A patch with one fix: **App hosts stop flapping to Cloudflare 526.** The LakeApp controller's Secret pass deleted
the App Ingress's certificate copy and edge client CA on every pass, and the Ingress step recreated them. Between the
two, ingress-nginx served its fake certificate and Cloudflare (Full strict) answered 526.

| Tests | see [Verification](#verification) |

## What it includes

- **The App's Secret pass keeps its Ingress's Secrets.** `deleteStaleLakeAppSecrets` deleted every
  Secret with the App's labels that its `wanted` set didn't name, including the certificate copies
  (`orama.cloud/tls-copy=true`) and `<workload>-edge-client-ca`, which `reconcileLakeAppIngress` labels
  the same way. It now skips both; `deleteLakeAppTLS` and the copy cleanup still remove them when the
  host goes. Present since the edge client CA joined App Ingresses (v0.6.2); every runtime App with a
  host was affected.

## Upgrading

No CRD or schema change. Roll the managing cluster's operator, then every leaf's `OramaOperator` to `v0.10.5`.
The App Secrets stop being recreated on the operator's next pass; nothing else needs doing.
