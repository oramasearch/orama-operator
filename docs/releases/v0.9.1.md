# v0.9.1

**Released:** 2026-10-24 · **Branch:** `main` · **Previous:** [v0.9.0](v0.9.0.md) (2026-10-23).

A patch carrying **what lake v0.5.0 needs from the operator**: its schema through
`0098_authentication_attempts`, the Clerk secret key, and the Clerk and Stripe webhook events lake now
handles. Everything in v0.9.0 is unchanged.

> **Read [Before it rolls](#before-it-rolls).** This operator rolls before the lake v0.5.0 image, and
> migration `0094` signs every Clerk-linked account out once.

## Before it rolls

- **Roll it before the lake v0.5.0 image.** lake v0.4.0 keeps running on the new schema.
- **Migration `0094` revokes every session and device of a Clerk-linked account once**: everyone signs
  in again after the schema Job runs. All data is test data.
- **Migration `0097` adds a unique index on `cloud_orders.stripe_subscription_id`**: the schema Job
  fails if two orders already share a subscription id.
- **Each Lake restarts once** when `LAKE_CLERK_SECRET_KEY` appears in its Deployment.
- **zetta v0.5.0** still needs this operator line (v0.9.0 or later) rolled first, for
  `ZETTA_CONNECTOR_ALLOW_DESTINATIONS`.
- Rolling needs Steve's consent: the managing cluster by its script, then every leaf's
  `operator.yaml` in one command.

## What it does not include

- Suspending resources from `cloud_order_desired_state` ([#897](https://github.com/oramasearch/orama-operator/issues/897): sleep, never delete).
- A NetworkPolicy keeping in-cluster callers off lake's HTTP port ([lake#1083](https://github.com/oramasearch/lake/issues/1083)).
- The pull requests on hold in v0.9.0 (#727, #730, #857, #860, #864).
