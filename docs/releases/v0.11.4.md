# v0.11.4

**Released:** 2026-10-30 · **Branch:** `main` · **Previous:** [v0.11.3](v0.11.3.md) (2026-10-30).

A patch release: **kube-prometheus-stack no longer raises `KubeCPUQuotaOvercommit` and `KubeMemoryQuotaOvercommit`** on any
cluster. They fire when the namespaces' quotas add up to more than 1.5 times the nodes. Each Lake workspace gets a
`workspace-defaults` quota of 4 CPU and 8 GiB, a ceiling and not a reservation, so the quotas are meant to add up to more than
the cluster: on one nonprod cluster, 19 workspaces came to 4.9 times its CPU and 2.9 times its memory.

## What it includes

- Two more entries in the `defaultRules.disabled` list from v0.11.3, set the same way in `helmfile.yaml` and every leaf's
  `config/clusters/*/platform.yaml`, with the reason beside them and in
  [observability.md](../infrastructure/observability.md). A workspace that reaches its own quota still alerts
  (`KubeQuotaExceeded`, warning; `KubeQuotaFullyUsed` and `KubeQuotaAlmostFull`, info), and a pod that cannot be placed
  pages as `KubePodNotReady` after 15 minutes.

## What it does not include

- Any change to quotas, node pools, requests or other rules.
- Pixie itself.

## Upgrading

- No CRD changes. The CRD rollout ([crd-rollout.md](../operations/crd-rollout.md)) runs as for any release and changes nothing.
- **The platform values:** the leaves take them when their `ClusterHelmRelease`s are applied from
  `config/clusters/*/platform.yaml`; the managing cluster with `helmfile sync -l name=kube-prometheus-stack`. Only the
  `PrometheusRule` holding the rules changes; Prometheus reloads its rules without restarting.
- **What rolls on each cluster with the operator:** the operator, and the zetta-tunnel mesh pods, which carry a container
  from the operator image. No Lake or Cloud v1 workload changes.
